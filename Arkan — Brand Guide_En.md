# Arkan — Brand Guide

> Guide to Arkan’s visual and verbal brand identity. This document is the reference for all design, website, and chatbot applications.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

**Positioning:** A mature and trusted advisor for businesses that want to grow the right way — sustainably, not quickly or recklessly.

**Brand Tagline:** **The Pillars of Sustainable Growth**

**Archetype:** **The Sage** with a touch of **The Caregiver** — knowledgeable, calm, and always alongside the client.

### Mission

We help small and medium-sized businesses build sustainable, measurable growth through clear strategy and disciplined execution — not fast, high-risk growth, but growth that lasts.

### Vision

To become the most trusted growth partner for Iranian businesses — the place every founder can confidently turn to when facing major business decisions.

### Brand Values

1. **Honesty Before the Contract** — We tell the truth, even when it means losing a deal.
2. **Execution, Not Just Advice** — We stay alongside our clients until plans become results.
3. **Decisions Based on Reality** — We make decisions based on data and the real conditions of the business, not assumptions or slogans.
4. **Respect for the Client’s Time & Capital** — Every recommendation should create more value than it costs.
5. **Sustainable Growth Over Shortcuts** — We build solutions designed to last for the long term.

---

## 2. Brand Personality & Tone

**Personality:** Expert, calm, trustworthy, direct, warm yet professional.

### Tone of Voice Principles

- Direct and clear; avoid complicated or overly sophisticated language.
- Reassuring without exaggeration or empty promises. We never guarantee success.
- Respectful and professional.
- Short, action-oriented sentences. Every sentence should communicate one clear idea.

### Say / Don’t Say

- ✅ **“We’ll contact you within 24 business hours.”**
- ❌ “Click now and transform your life!!!”
- ✅ **“We stay alongside you through implementation.”**
- ❌ “Iran’s best and unrivalled consultants.”

---

## 3. Logo

**Structure:** A Persian wordmark, **“Arkan” (آرکان)**, paired with a simple symbol consisting of four vertical lines with coordinated heights, representing the **four pillars**.

### Versions

- **Full version (symbol + wordmark)** — default use
- **Symbol only** — for avatars, favicons, and stamps
- **Monochrome** — Pine Green on light backgrounds / Bone on dark backgrounds

### Rules

- Clear space around the logo must be at least equal to the height of one line in the symbol.
- Minimum digital display width: **120px**
- Do not rotate, add shadows, change colours, stretch, or compress the logo.

## 4. Colour Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Pine | `#143A32` | Dark backgrounds, header, footer, primary CTA |
| Accent | Brass | `#B5853A` | Links, icons, accent lines, gold details |
| Light Base | Bone | `#F7F3EC` | Main page backgrounds |
| Secondary | Sand | `#E7DECF` | Cards and secondary surfaces |
| Primary Text | Ink | `#15201C` | Body text on light backgrounds |
| Secondary Text | Slate | `#5A5F5B` | Subheadings and secondary text |
| White | White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark backgrounds and surfaces |

### 60 / 30 / 10 Rule

- **60%** — Light tones (Bone / White)
- **30%** — Pine
- **10%** — Brass

### Accessibility — Important

- **Primary CTA:** Pine background + Bone text for high contrast.
- Do not use Brass for small text on white backgrounds, as the contrast is insufficient. Brass should be reserved for links, icons, accent lines, and larger surfaces.

## 5. Typography

| Role | Font | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Headings | **Estedad** | Modern, clean, distinctive |
| Body | **Vazirmatn** | Readable and professional |
| Numbers | Tabular figures | For statistics and numerical data |

> Both fonts are free and can be loaded via CDN (jsDelivr).

### Desktop Type Scale

- **H1:** 48px / Weight 700 / Line-height 1.2
- **H2:** 32px / Weight 700
- **H3:** 24px / Weight 600
- **Body:** 17px / Weight 400 / Line-height 1.8
- **Caption:** 14px / Slate

### Language Direction

- **Persian version:** RTL (Right-to-Left)
- **English version:** LTR (Left-to-Right)
- Both language versions should share the same design system and visual identity.
- Persian numerals may be used in Persian body copy.
- Latin numerals should be used in English content, forms, and contact information.

---

## 6. Layout & Surface Principles

- Use a **12-column grid** with appropriate page margins.
- **Generous white space** — a mature brand is built through visual breathing room, not clutter.
- Soft corners: **12px radius** for cards and **8px** for buttons.
- Shadows should be extremely subtle and low-contrast; avoid glossy effects and strong gradients.
- Each section should have **one message and one purpose**. Keep it focused.

---

## 7. Buttons & Forms

### Primary Button

- Pine background
- Bone text
- 8px corner radius
- On hover: slightly darker with a Brass underline

### Secondary Button

- Pine border
- Transparent background

### Form Fields

- White background
- Thin Slate border
- Brass border on focus
- Labels positioned above the fields

---

## 8. Visual Style

- Use **real, warm photography** of workplaces, meetings, and conversations — not cliché stock photography such as artificial handshakes or meaningless charts.
- Alternatively, use **minimal line illustrations** with Brass accents.
- Overall tone: **calm, professional, human.**

---

## 9. Things to Avoid

- No loud or overly saturated colours.
- No strong gradients.
- No excessive animation.
- No advertising-heavy language.
- No repeated exclamation marks.
- No guaranteed-success claims.
- No visual clutter or lack of white space.
- Do not use Brass for small text on white backgrounds.
